# Server Rules

Welcome to the Cosy Discord! Please read and follow these rules to keep this community a friendly and helpful place for everyone.

## General Conduct
- Be respectful and kind to others. No harassment, hate speech, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions civil, even when you disagree.
- No spamming, excessive self-promotion, or unsolicited DMs.

## Channels
- Use channels for their intended purpose. Check the channel description if you're unsure.
- Keep support questions in the appropriate support channels.

## Content
- No NSFW content of any kind.
- Do not share malicious software, exploits, or pirated content.
- Do not share personal information of others.

## Cosy & Game Servers
- Do not share credentials, tokens, or secrets in any channel. If you accidentally post them, rotate them immediately.
- When asking for help, provide relevant details (installation method, logs, error messages) so others can assist you.

## Enforcement
Moderators reserve the right to remove messages and restrict access at their discretion. Repeated or severe violations may result in a ban.
